Research

Reliability and Explainability of Vision Transformers Under Medical Distribution Shift: A Comparative Study on Chest X-Ray Classification

Sosna W. Achamyeleh · In preparation · Target venue: WiCV @ ECCV 2026

The ViT outperforms the CNN in distribution but degrades more sharply once the data shifts. Calibration breaks down for both, Monte Carlo dropout fails to flag out-of-distribution inputs, and attention rollout and GradCAM localize poorly under shift, raising the question of whether these explanations can be trusted when they matter most.
